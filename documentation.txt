5. How to Use the App (User Instructions)

Run:

python image_annotator.py


Click Load Image

Choose a tool

Draw on the image using mouse

For Measure, click two points to see distance in pixels

Click Save Image



7. Limitations

Measurement is in pixels only

No undo/redo

No zoom or pan

One image at a time
